[E01] <p>You can claim back 25p every time an individual donates £1 to your charity or community amateur sports club (CASC). This is called Gift Aid.</p>
[E02] <p>You must be recognised as a charity or CASC for tax purposes.</p>
[E03] <p>The donor must:</p>
[E04] <li>have paid at least as much in Income Tax or Capital Gains Tax in that tax year as you want to claim in Gift Aid</li>
[E05] <li>make a Gift Aid declaration that gives you permission to claim it</li>
[E06] <p>You can claim Gift Aid on donations from individuals. The donor must:</p>
[E07] <li>have paid the same amount or more in Income Tax or Capital Gains Tax in that tax year</li>
[E08] <p>You cannot claim on donations:</p>
[E09] <li>made through Payroll Giving</li>
[E10] <li>that are a payment for goods or services or made because your charity or CASC bought goods and services</li>
[E11] <li>where the donor gets a ‘benefit’ over a certain limit</li>
[E12] <li>of shares</li>
[E13] <li>from charity cards or of vouchers, for example Charities Aid Foundation (CAF) vouchers</li>

=== DECISION_QUESTION ===
Am I eligible for Gift Aid?

=== CUSTOMER_SCENARIO ===
I run a small, independent charity which attempts to help people struggling with their vet costs. I have several people who pay monthly by direct debit and also receive some one off donations.

Additional customer information:
The charity's HMRC recognition was confirmed as still in force on 16 March 2026. Elaine Carter sent £120 from her own bank account on 16 March 2026 (FP-0316-EL) after reading an emergency veterinary appeal. Her signed Gift Aid declaration covers this payment. Her Income Tax paid for 2025–26 was £980, and the Gift Aid claimed for this and her other donations that year totals £80. The fundraiser checked the associated sales and booking records: Elaine received no goods, services or benefit in exchange. This was not a payroll deduction or a transfer of shares. The processor's CAF charity-card and voucher receipts were different payments with different settlement references; FP-0316-EL was Elaine's direct bank transfer.
