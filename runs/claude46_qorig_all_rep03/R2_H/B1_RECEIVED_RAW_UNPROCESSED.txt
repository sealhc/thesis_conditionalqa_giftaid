{"decision":"NO","supporting_policy_ids":["E08","E10","E11"],"missing_facts":[],"justification":"The £260 payment from Robyn Ellis on 4 February 2026 cannot qualify for Gift Aid. Although it is labelled 'donation' internally, the evidence clearly shows it is a payment for goods or services: Robyn requested two advertising placements (a booking link in the newsletter and a six-week homepage feature), the coordinator quoted exactly £260 for those placements, Robyn confirmed the amount 'worked for her', the booking sheet moved from 'awaiting settlement' to 'confirmed' when the £260 arrived, and the placements subsequently appeared in the agreed locations. The fact that no invoice was raised and a volunteer relabelled it a 'donation' does not change the economic substance. Under E08/E10, Gift Aid cannot be claimed on donations 'that are a payment for goods or services'. The £260 is plainly such a payment. Even if one argued there was some donative element, Robyn received a direct commercial benefit (advertising exposure) whose value equals the full amount paid, which would also fall foul of E11 (donor receives a benefit over the permitted limit).
