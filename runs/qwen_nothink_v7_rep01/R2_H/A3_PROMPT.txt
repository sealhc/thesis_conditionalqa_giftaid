[E01] <p>You can claim back 25p every time an individual donates £1 to your charity or community amateur sports club (CASC). This is called Gift Aid.</p>
[E02] <p>You must be recognised as a charity or CASC for tax purposes.</p>
[E03] <p>The donor must:</p>
[E04] <li>have paid at least as much in Income Tax or Capital Gains Tax in that tax year as you want to claim in Gift Aid</li>
[E05] <li>make a Gift Aid declaration that gives you permission to claim it</li>
[E06] <p>You can claim Gift Aid on donations from individuals. The donor must:</p>
[E07] <li>have paid the same amount or more in Income Tax or Capital Gains Tax in that tax year</li>
[E08] <p>You cannot claim on donations:</p>
[E09] <li>made through Payroll Giving</li>
[E10] <li>that are a payment for goods or services or made because your charity or CASC bought goods and services</li>
[E11] <li>where the donor gets a ‘benefit’ over a certain limit</li>
[E12] <li>of shares</li>
[E13] <li>from charity cards or of vouchers, for example Charities Aid Foundation (CAF) vouchers</li>

=== DECISION_QUESTION ===
Am I eligible for Gift Aid?

The coordinator's booking sheet shows the placements as 'awaiting settlement' until 4 February, when the £260 arrived; the same sheet then changed to 'confirmed'. A draft plan to cancel the March newsletter appears in the folder, but it is marked 'not approved'; the final distribution log and archived newsletter show that the edition went out. We have the charity's HMRC recognition letter. Robyn's personal tax position and whether she completed a Gift Aid form for the £260 have not been checked. The receipt under review in this file is the February £260 entry, not her separate £20 collection gift.
